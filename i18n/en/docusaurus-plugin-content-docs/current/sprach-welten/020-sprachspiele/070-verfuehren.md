<!-- # The Language Game of Seduction -->

<!--t src=73422a50-->
## Real-life examples

<!--t src=fc6fe98e-->
Seduction as a linguistic game of enticement pervades countless everyday situations:

<!--t src=689ca56b-->
- The friend who persuades us to have dessert with the description of a "divine dessert";
- the salesperson who describes the scent of the new car as the "essence of freedom";
- the app that suggests the "last available room with a sea view";
- the friend who whispers: "No one will find out";
- the advertisement that promises a product will make us more attractive, more successful or happier;
- the political speaker who paints a too-beautiful vision of the future;
- the child who, wide-eyed, begs to "stay up just five minutes longer";
- the charismatic company leader who fires people up for his vision of a risky project.
- and not least the classic of the man courting a woman, or vice versa if you prefer.

<!--t src=3ce4fe61-->
## What it aims at

<!--t src=6167172a-->
The language game of seduction aims at blurring the boundaries between **reason** and desire and at getting the other to do or think something that goes **against their usual principles** or **against their better judgement**. Unlike mere persuading, it is less about rational arguments than about addressing deeper-lying longings, **hidden wishes** and **unadmitted desires**. Seduction awakens a desire or intensifies it and at the same time makes the inhibitions standing between the desire and its fulfilment seem less important. It is a subtle game with possibilities and **fantasies**, a dance on the boundary between the permitted and the forbidden.

<!--t src=e9c44be0-->
## Success criteria

<!--t src=f9eff7c4-->
A successful seduction is marked by the fact that:

<!--t src=de779a6d-->
- It overcomes or temporarily suspends the inner conflicts of the addressee
- It creates a feeling of urgency or of "now or never"
- It cleverly minimizes or disguises the possible negative consequences
- It subtly uses the appeal of the forbidden or the unusual
- It gives the seduced the feeling of having made the decision themselves
- It offers an experience felt to be worthwhile &mdash; even if regret follows later
- It hits a moment of weakness or receptiveness precisely

<!--t src=b51764d5-->
A seduction is especially masterful when the seduced, even after rational reflection, comes to the conclusion that giving in was the right decision &mdash; although it violated original principles.

<!--t src=f899b6a7-->
## How it can go wrong

<!--t src=84c50a5f-->
Seduction can fail when:

<!--t src=e97c78d8-->
- The addressee's moral principles or self-control are underestimated
- The enticement is presented too directly or too clumsily and thereby activates defence mechanisms
- The possible consequences appear all too obviously negative
- The timing is unfavourable and there is no emotional openness
- The seducer seems untrustworthy or his intention is too transparent
- The social situation makes giving in impossible (e.g. the presence of other people)
- The seduction turns into manipulation and is recognized as such

<!--t src=903fea01-->
A seduction fails especially catastrophically when it is not only rejected but also provokes distrust or disgust toward the seducer.

<!--t src=4775c5b9-->
## How it is abused

<!--t src=d3c5b2b7-->
Seduction can be deployed in an ethically problematic way:

<!--t src=6d41296a-->
- To bring people to decisions that harm them in the long term
- Through the deliberate exploitation of emotional weaknesses or dependencies
- To switch off critical judgement, especially in suggestible people
- Through the targeted awakening of unrealistic expectations or false promises
- For manipulation in asymmetrical power relationships
- To cross social boundaries or violate taboo zones
- Through creating an illusion of voluntariness in what are actually manipulative processes

<!--t src=7142bbc8-->
Especially in marketing, politics, destructive cults and toxic personal relationships, the dark sides of seduction strategies show themselves, aiming more at exploitation than at mutual gain.

<!--t src=947d7d94-->
## Social role

<!--t src=a245e261-->
The language game of seduction fulfils complex social functions:

<!--t src=fd6a741a-->
- It allows socially accepted ways of handling taboos and norms flexibly
- It enables the playful sounding-out of boundaries and the exploration of the forbidden
- It serves as a catalyst for change and for transgressing the habitual
- It enriches social interactions with tension, fantasy and emotional intensity
- It enables a "time-out" from the strict rules of everyday rationality
- It enables a change of perspective and the experiencing of new possibilities
- It forms a cultural motif in art, literature and film (Faust, Don Juan etc.)

<!--t src=32518756-->
In its constructive form, seduction can help people to overcome self-imposed boundaries, make new experiences and break out of stuck patterns. The ambivalence of seduction &mdash; between liberating breakout and manipulative overwhelming &mdash; makes it one of the most fascinating and ethically most complex language games we know.
