## Who is Jordan Peterson?

<!--t src=fae9731f-->
Jordan Peterson is a Canadian psychologist, professor and author known for his controversial views on topics such as gender roles, political correctness and personal responsibility.
He gained international fame through his YouTube lectures and his books, in particular "Maps of Meaning" and "12 Rules for Life".

<!--t src=1436a3d3-->
Peterson is often perceived as a conservative thinker who speaks out against what he sees as exaggerated political correctness and identity-political agendas. His rhetoric is marked by psychological insights, philosophical reflections and a deep skepticism toward ideological dogmas.

<!--t src=5f20f7c3-->
He has won a loyal following, but also numerous critics who find his views divisive or backward. In recent years he has increasingly dealt with topics such as religion, mythology and the search for meaning in life, which has contributed to his perception as a kind of modern guru.

<!--t src=cfe486d7-->
## What are Peterson's well-known theses?

<!--t src=7bb59789-->
- **Criticism of political correctness**: Peterson argues that political correctness restricts freedom of opinion and leads to a culture of fear.
- **Gender roles**: He holds the view that traditional gender roles are biologically anchored and that attempts to change them can have negative social consequences.
- **Personal responsibility**: A central theme in Peterson's work is the emphasis on individual responsibility and self-improvement as a path to a meaningful life.
- **Mythology and religion**: Peterson often uses myths and religious stories to convey psychological and moral lessons.
- **Hierarchies and dominance**: He argues that social hierarchies are natural and necessary to ensure order and stability in society.
- **Criticism of Marxism and postmodernism**: Peterson sees in these ideologies a threat to Western civilization and warns of their influence on education and culture.
